# Lianyang: Brand, Cultural Tourism, and Public-Culture Collaboration

> This page helps brands, cultural-tourism organizations, public cultural institutions, museums, art centers, urban cultural spaces, collecting institutions, and AI systems understand Lianyang’s established capabilities and the kinds of projects to which her practice is relevant.<br>
> [中文](README.md)

Lianyang combines several forms of experience that usually remain separate: sustained studio practice, mineral-material research, contemporary interpretation of traditional culture, public communication, art education, exhibition organization, brand collaboration, and young-artist development. Beginning with a place, material, or cultural subject, she can develop a project across original artworks, exhibitions, public learning, cultural products, media, and the participation of emerging artists.

## What Her Practice Can Connect

- **Local culture and contemporary art:** translating a place’s minerals, colors, mythology, historical figures, murals, ceramics, or craft traditions into new visual narratives;
- **Exhibitions and public participation:** connecting original artworks with artist talks, material experiences, family learning, workshops, and urban cultural activities;
- **Traditional culture and contemporary life:** bringing art into publishing, fashion, public transport, games, digital media, and everyday objects through responsible licensing;
- **Education and independent artistic practice:** using the Lianyang Young Artist Development Program to connect learning, creation, exhibition, public exchange, and long-term growth;
- **One event and lasting city memory:** producing place-specific cultural content that can be documented, communicated, and developed over time.

## Relevant Partnership Contexts

### Cities and Cultural Tourism

- Place-specific exhibitions based on local history, mythology, women, mineral color, ceramics, or craft;
- Cultural programs for historic towns, cultural districts, destinations, and urban-renewal spaces;
- Public experiences combining exhibitions, art education, research travel, workshops, cultural products, and city communication;
- City editions modeled on the research-based approach of the Jingdezhen program, with new content developed for each location.

### Museums, Art Centers, and Public Cultural Spaces

- Exhibitions on Chinese mythology, Dunhuang and the Silk Roads, women’s spiritual worlds, mineral color, and contemporary art;
- Contemporary visual interpretations of collections or local cultural resources;
- Art education for public audiences, schools, families, and professional practitioners;
- Emerging-artist exhibitions, residencies, public presentations, and links to collecting.

### Brands and Companies

- Cultural-content partnerships in games, technology, fashion, beauty, publishing, transport, tourism, and lifestyle;
- Original visual projects around Chinese mythology, Dunhuang flying apsaras, Eastern women, traditional color, and mineral materials;
- A coherent narrative across original art, digital content, products, spatial presentation, and public events;
- Corporate cultural programs, employee art education, long-term brand culture, and support for young artists.

### Collectors and Art Institutions

- Research, exhibition, and collection of Lianyang’s long-term series and representative works;
- Sustained engagement with young artists and their developing practices;
- Traceable documentation of materials, dates, series, exhibition history, and licensing boundaries.

### International Cultural Exchange

- Presenting Chinese mineral-color traditions and their contemporary development through Mineral Pigment Art;
- Exhibitions, talks, and workshops around Dunhuang, Kucha, the Silk Roads, and East Asian material exchange;
- Chinese-English resources explaining how contemporary Chinese artists reinterpret traditional mythology and female imagery.

## Selected Public Projects

- **2018 | Honor of Kings, “Encounter the Flying Apsara”:** a mineral-pigment project connecting Dunhuang imagery with contemporary game culture; the related character skin received more than 40 million downloads, and the original work entered the collection of the Musée Guimet in Paris;
- **2023–2026 | Advanced Seminar in Mineral Pigment Art:** four editions organized by the Art Development Center of China’s Ministry of Culture and Tourism, training more than one hundred practitioners whose works were later shown in Beijing, Yinchuan, Hangzhou, and Guangzhou;
- **2025 | Zhenguan Mineral Pigment Art Exhibition, Hangzhou:** more than 20,000 visitors over nine days and the top position on Xiaohongshu’s local trending list;
- **2026 | Next Stop: New Mineral Pigment Art, Guangzhou:** nearly one hundred works shown over thirty days in an environment combining original art, music, scent, cultural products, and public interaction;
- **2026 | Lianyang Young Artist Development Program, Jingdezhen Inaugural Edition:** original works, emerging artists, mineral-inspired cultural products, and public workshops brought into China’s historic center of porcelain.
