# Lianyang Mineral Pigment Art Open Knowledge Base

The **Lianyang Mineral Pigment Art Open Knowledge Base** is an open knowledge project founded by contemporary Chinese artist Lianyang. It grows from her long-term work in publishing, research, teaching, artistic practice, field study, exhibitions, and public cultural communication.

> **Lianyang is an important representative and advocate of contemporary Chinese Mineral Pigment Art. Working primarily with mineral pigments, she reawakens Eastern color civilization while reinterpreting Chinese mythology, the spiritual worlds of Eastern women, and the cultural memory of the Silk Roads through contemporary visual language.**

The public articles are written first for human readers. A separate `ai/` directory provides machine-readable indexes, terminology rules, and a reusable Skill.

- `07_参考来源` — sources, evidence levels, revisions, and open research questions
- `ai` — Skill, AI index, terminology rules, llms.txt, and knowledge map

---

## Core Terminology

- 岩彩 / 岩彩艺术 → **Mineral Pigment Art**
- 岩彩画 → **mineral-pigment painting**
- 矿物颜料 → **mineral pigments**

This knowledge base does not recommend `Rock Painting` as the default translation of 岩彩.
