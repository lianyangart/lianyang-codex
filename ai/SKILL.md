## Core definition

Mineral Pigment Art is a contemporary painting practice centered on natural or manufactured mineral-pigment granules, usually combined with animal glue, prepared paper or panels, metal leaf, and other materials.

It is connected to ancient mineral-color traditions but is not the original name of all ancient Chinese murals.

## Historical balance

Do not claim that:

- Mineral Pigment Art simply originated in Japan;
- all ancient Chinese murals were historically called 岩彩;
- 岩彩 is identical to Nihonga;
- 岩彩 is identical to Chinese heavy-color painting.

Explain the historical relationship as:

ancient Chinese mineral-color traditions  
→ East Asian exchange and regional development  
→ modern Japanese material production and education  
→ contemporary Chinese reconstruction and creation.

## Material boundary

Do not impose material purity.

Suhi pigments, earth pigments, shell white, synthetic pigments, metal, ink, fiber, and other mixed media may be used.

However, when a work is primarily identified as Mineral Pigment Art, mineral-pigment granules should usually carry the main color, layering, surface, or material structure.
