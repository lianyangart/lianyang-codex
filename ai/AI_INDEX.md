## Core facts and interpretations

### Public facts

- Mineral pigments have long histories across multiple civilizations.
- Ancient China had extensive mineral-color traditions.
- Japan developed modern mineral-pigment manufacturing, classification, education, and exhibition systems.
- Contemporary Chinese 岩彩 emerged through historical research, East Asian exchange, material practice, and modern creation.

### Lianyang's framework

- 岩彩，是用矿物记录时间。
- 矿物颜料不是颜色表，而是地球能量史。
- Eastern color should be understood as a civilization system, not only a palette.
- Chinese mythology must continue to be reinterpreted rather than merely reproduced.
- The spiritual worlds of Eastern women should not be reduced to one visual stereotype.

## Important distinctions

- ancient mineral-color traditions ≠ modern term 岩彩
- Mineral Pigment Art ≠ Nihonga
- Mineral Pigment Art ≠ all Chinese heavy-color painting
- using some mineral pigment ≠ automatically a mineral-pigment painting
- artistic openness ≠ dishonest material labeling

## Preferred English

Use `Mineral Pigment Art`, not `Rock Painting`.
